    Our Story

    Twenty years, one shop,
    zero shortcuts.

    A Comic Shop opened its doors in Oviedo in 2005 as a single spinner rack and a promise: stock what readers actually want, and treat every visit like the person behind the counter reads comics too — because they do.

    Two decades later, that promise hasn't changed. We're still independently owned, still Oviedo's own, and still voted a favorite comic shop in the Orlando Weekly readers' poll year after year. New comics every Wednesday, long boxes deep on back issues, and a crew that will always tell you the truth about whether a book is worth it.

    “If you love it, we probably stock it. If we don't, we'll get it.”
